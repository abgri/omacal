OmaCal Brian Privacy Notice

This notice describes the personal macOS build of OmaCal Brian maintained by Brian Acklen.

Google Calendar data

With your permission, the app accesses your Google Calendar account and calendar information, including event titles, descriptions, times, locations, organizers, attendees and meeting links. It uses this information to display calendars, synchronize changes, manage events and invitations, and provide reminders. Changes you make are sent to Google Calendar; invitations and updates may be shared with event participants through Google.

Local storage

The app stores account information, calendar data, settings and synchronization information in a local database on your Mac. Google refresh tokens are stored in macOS Keychain. Calendar synchronization connects directly to Google rather than through a separate OmaCal Brian server.

Other connections

If used, CalDAV, CardDAV and subscribed-calendar features connect to the providers you configure. Online place search sends typed location searches to Photon. Weather features contact Open-Meteo and may contact wttr.in to determine an approximate location from your IP address. Release checks contact GitHub. Opening meeting or other external links connects you to the relevant service. These services receive the requests necessary for those features, including your IP address.

Advertising and analytics

This build does not implement advertising or analytics tracking, or send calendar data to an OmaCal Brian-operated collection service.

Your controls

Signing out removes the account’s cached data from the app’s database and attempts to remove its stored credential and revoke Google access. You can also revoke access in your Google Account settings. Signing out does not delete your calendars from Google. Device backups or other copies may retain previously stored data.

This website

These information pages do not access your calendar. They are hosted by GitHub Pages, which logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Contact

Use the project’s GitHub issues for questions. Issues are public; do not include calendar contents, credentials or other private information.
